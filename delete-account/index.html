Delete your Boutiques account

In the app

  1. Open Boutiques and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email cto@fundedarmy.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the mobile number or Google account you signed in with. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (mobile number or Google account) and profile
  • Boutiques you listed, with their outfits, photos and videos
  • Your saved outfits, notifications and the devices registered for push notifications

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Shoppers who saved one of your outfits lose that entry too. Anonymous server logs that do not identify you are deleted automatically after a short time.
