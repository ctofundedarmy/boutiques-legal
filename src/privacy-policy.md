**Last updated: 28 October 2026**

Boutiques is made by FundedArmy ("we", "us"). It helps people in Rewari, Haryana find local boutiques and their outfits. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how to delete it.

Questions? Email **raopawan0309@gmail.com**.

## What we collect

### If you only browse

You can browse boutiques and outfits without signing in. Then we collect only:

- **Crash reports.** If the app crashes, a report goes to Sentry (see below). It has the device model, Android version, app version, a random installation ID and what the app was doing. It has no name or email.
- **Your location, only if you allow it.** See "Location" below.

Your language choice and other settings stay on your phone.

### If you sign in

Sign-in uses your Google account. We receive your Google name, email address and profile photo and store them in your profile. We never see your Google password. Your email is visible only to you and to our admins. Other users never see it.

Signed-in shoppers can save outfits. We store the list of outfits you saved.

### If you list a boutique

Shop owners enter the shop name, area, address, phone number, WhatsApp number, opening hours and a description. They upload photos and videos of outfits, with prices. All of this is public. Every user of the app can see it, and shoppers can call or message the shop directly. Only upload what you want to be public.

We also record review decisions on your boutique (approved, rejected and why, suspended, reinstated) and send you a notification about them.

### Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app saves a push token for your device. We use it to tell you about your boutique's review. Push messages are delivered by Expo's push service. Signing out removes the token.

## Location

The app asks for location permission to sort outfits by distance and to show boutiques near you on the map. It uses your location only while the app is open on screen, never in the background. Your coordinates travel with the request so the server can sort by distance. They are not stored. You can say no. The app still works and sorts by newest instead. You can turn location off at any time in Android settings.

## Who processes the data

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. These companies process data for us:

| Service | What for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Google (Google Sign-In) | Signing you in with your Google account | Google's servers; Google's privacy policy applies |
| Supabase | Our database, file storage and sign-in sessions | Mumbai, India (AWS region ap-south-1) |
| Sentry | Crash reports | Sentry's servers outside India (United States or European Union) |
| Expo | Delivering push notifications | Expo's servers (United States) |

## How long we keep data

- Your profile, saved outfits, listings and notifications stay while your account exists. They are deleted when you delete the account.
- Push tokens are deleted when you sign out or delete the account.
- Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
- Server logs that do not identify you are deleted automatically after a short time.
- Copies in database backups are removed within 30 days of deletion.

## Deleting your account

**In the app:** open the **Account** tab, tap **Delete my account**, then confirm. The account and everything linked to it are deleted straight away: your sign-in and profile, boutiques you listed with their outfits, photos and videos, your saved outfits, your notifications and push tokens.

**By email:** if you no longer have the app, email **raopawan0309@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my account" from the Google account you signed in with, or tell us which account it was. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

Deletion is permanent. Shoppers who saved one of your outfits lose that entry too.

## Children

Boutiques is for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over HTTPS. Only a boutique's owner can change its listings. Only admins can review boutiques. Access to the database is limited to the FundedArmy team.

## Changes to this policy

When something changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Big changes are also announced in the app.
